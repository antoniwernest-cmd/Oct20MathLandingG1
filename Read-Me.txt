MY LEARNING GARDEN — GRADE 1

OPEN AND DEPLOY
Open Learning-Garden-Deployment/index.html. Deploy that folder as a static website. Its index.html contains the pictures, font, scripts and styles, so it also works as a standalone local file. Grade-1-Learning-Garden.html is an identical standalone copy. No student ID is required. No ZIP is provided.
Refresh an already-open browser tab after an update.

735 ACTIVITIES
There are 21 sections: 11 Math sections and 10 Language sections. Beginning sounds has 90 pages, Listen to sentences has 60, Thoughts & feelings has 45, and the other sections each have 30 activities. Use the Math/Language switch on the landing page. Every activity heading has a picture. Previous, Next and the question selector allow repeated practice.

MATH — 330 ACTIVITIES
1. Add block sets: join pictured blocks; drag or tap a total up to 20.
2. Hundreds chart addition: 30 count-on questions with totals up to 20, a full 1–100 chart, coloured counter groups and three total choices. Tap any chart number to turn its yellow light on or off. The blue outline marks the starting number. Clear lights resets the chart; Hear directions reads the learner steps. Chart lights save with the response and appear in PNG/Excel exports. Arrow keys move between chart numbers.
3. Compare & order: compare groups and arrange numbers up to 50. The ordering board has large number cards with a finger and Drag me cue, plus one clean row of First/Next/Last drop spaces. Drag into any space; move or swap placed numbers, or return them to the tray. Placed cards disappear from the tray. Start again resets the board. The 50 chart button switches to a full-size support chart; Numbers returns to the cards without changing the answer. The same pointer-drag handling supports mouse, touch and stylus. Tap controls remain available.
4. Count to 50: count by 1s, 5s and 10s using a chart, grouped counters or a number line. Drag a number into the active empty space, or tap it.
5. Add & subtract: short stories with matching apple, car, ball, flower or crayon pictures, one picture for each object. Addition uses a group that can be dragged to Put together. Every subtraction question starts with all pictures together in one group; each picture is individually draggable. Drag any objects into Put away, one at a time, and drag them back to undo. The moved count updates as the student works. The instruction changes to Count what is left when the requested number has been removed; over-removal asks the student to drag pictures back. Checking verifies both the number removed and the answer. Answers are visible immediately and can be dragged into the equation. No setup button is required. Tapping an individual picture remains an alternative. Totals stay within 20. Saved group moves from the earlier version are represented as individual objects; earlier subtraction checks are flagged for review.
6. Repeating patterns: 30 varied patterns use 28 distinct pictures: stars, hearts, triangles, diamonds, hexagons, moons, clouds, suns, flowers, butterflies, rockets, planets, kites, balloons, raindrops, rainbows, fruit, animals, cars, balls, crayons, pencils and blocks. Early pages alternate two pictures; later pages repeat pairs, grouped pieces or three pictures. Drag a piece into the highlighted missing position, then drag what comes next. There is no rule-card step. The instruction changes to the current step. Tap choices also work.
7. Sort & tell: drag shapes into a group, between groups, or back to the tray. Sorting uses one attribute: colour, shape, size or kind. Drag the sorting-rule card into My rule when the objects are placed. Tap alternatives remain available.
8. Make a graph: each data card shows the actual objects to count. The graph opens ready to build, with its correct title, labels, source and one-to-one key supplied. Drag each picture directly to its matching row; drag a placed unit back to its card to remove it. There are no title/source setup questions or Next button. Tapping pictures and the plus/minus controls are alternatives. Pictographs repeat the matching category picture: apples for apples, pears for pears, and so on. Concrete graphs use blocks with picture-supported category labels. Checking compares the displayed counts with the pictured data.
9. Read a graph: a simpler supported comparison of two small pictured groups, with up to five pictures per group. Drag the group with more into one large More space, or tap it. Each group shows its actual category pictures, label and count, plus a title, source and one-to-one key. This replaces the previous three-category ranking task.
10. Calendar time: read dates and identify days of the week.
11. Count & graph: 30 new two-bar activities. Count two types of mixed picture cards. Tap each card to mark it with the original Math Landing translucent selection band; tap again to unmark, or use Undo mark. The picture stays visible. Use the large plus/minus controls to build each bar on a fixed 0–6 scale. One block represents one card. When both bars match the cards, large picture or number answer buttons appear automatically for a more/fewer/count question. No extra reveal or next-step button is needed. Checking verifies both bars and the answer. Start again clears only this activity's marks, bars and answer, keeping teacher history. Cards, marks, bars and the answer save automatically and appear in PNG/Excel exports. The card-marking, translucent diagonal bands, graph controls and bounded one-unit increments are adapted from the Counting-On engine in Math Landing's extra-engines.js.

LANGUAGE — 405 ACTIVITIES
1. Letter names: identify capital/lowercase letters, in and out of order. The requested letter is shown in large type and always appears among the choices. Hear instructions and Hear letter use the actual target letter with a word example, such as A is for apple. Drag a matching choice to the model or tap it.
2. Alphabet order: large letter cards and drop spaces fill the available work area. Drag or tap letters into the spaces. Already-placed letters can be moved or swapped. The choice tray presents three large letters at a time. Spaces grow when the tray is empty, and the full alphabet also fits one screen.
3. Write letters: watch numbered stroke models and trace all 26 lowercase/capital pairs, with four review pairs. A pointing-finger picture and “Use your finger” cue support tracing. A mouse or stylus also works. Undo, Clear and paper practice remain available. Handwriting needs teacher observation; software does not score its quality.
4. Trace CVC words: 30 pages, using 15 pictured short words with a second pass for deliberate practice. A matching picture sits beside the large lowercase word. Finger, mouse and stylus ink is saved. Hear word, Watch strokes, Undo, Clear and Practise on paper use the existing tracing procedure. Teacher observation supports assessment.
5. Beginning sounds: 90 individual picture pages, including 60 added pages reusing checked existing artwork, each with six letter options. Drag or tap the matching first letter.
6. Letters & sounds: drag a picture to the displayed letter, or tap one of three pictures. Hear word reads each pictured word.
7. Listen to sentences: 60 pages, including 30 added pages reusing existing illustrations. Each page shows one large picture and one short sentence. Remix shuffles all 60 pages; Next and Previous follow that order. Pictures stay paired with their sentences, and saved listen counts stay with their original pages. The activity fills the screen. First letters stand out, and the current word highlights when its spoken audio begins. The book-and-speaker picture marks Read to me. Tap a word to hear it alone. A complete read-aloud records a listen count and marks the activity Finished. No quiz is required.
8. Story order: two picture cards, with clearly numbered 1 — First and 2 — Then spaces. A short two-event story stays visible and Read to me reads it. Drag or tap the pictures into order; placed cards can be moved or swapped. All 30 activities use two pictures.
9. Thoughts & feelings: 45 pages, including 15 new 3D animated-style scenes of children feeling sad after everyday disappointments. Each has a matching short read-aloud story. The picture fills most of the screen, beside a short two-sentence story, Read to me and one question, “How do you feel?” Choose happy, sad, calm or excited. On portrait screens, the picture sits above the story and four faces. A single feeling completes the response for teacher review. Personal feelings are never marked right or wrong.
10. Make a message: a large picture and one picture-word card support the frame “I see ___.” Drag or tap the card into the sentence, then use Read to me. There is no typing box or extra student step. Tools > Picture library offers all 200 reused scenes, six at a time, for an adult to choose another picture.

VISUALS AND AUDIO
Picture help on all 330 math activities opens a large pop-up using the existing math picture collection used by Math Landing. Pictures match the concept: counting, ordering, addition, subtraction, patterns, sorting and graphs. They are clearly labelled Picture example. Read to me reads just the short picture cue; Back to activity returns to the saved response. Calendar help enlarges the activity's actual calendar. Closing a dialog stops its voice. Math-Picture-Help contains the reused originals and Sources.txt. All picture help is also embedded in index.html for offline use.
Fifteen original feelings PNGs, matching deployment WEBPs, short stories and the exact built-in image-generation prompts are in Feelings-Art. Reused beginning-sound and CVC pictures are in Language-Pictures, with Sources.txt.
The original 200 scene illustrations come from Sentence Adventures and Sentences Adventures 2. The original harder passages are not used as learner text. New short Grade 1 sentences and stories support this program. The original 30 beginning-sound pictures remain.
Graph-Clip-Art contains 15 generated category pictures plus a pointing-finger picture, all with transparent backgrounds. The HTML embeds compact copies. Prompts.txt records the built-in image-generation prompts. Image-Prompts.txt records the original beginning-sound prompts.
Hear instructions speaks only student directions, without activity titles, page numbers, curriculum codes or teacher/admin controls. Letter-name directions identify the target directly. Read to me buttons have a book-and-speaker picture. CVC Hear word buttons say the exact displayed word, including after undo, clear or paper-practice redraws. Voice speed has five pictured choices: Very slow, Slow, Medium, Fast and Very fast. It stays beside Read to me in Listen to sentences, and is also available in Tools and Save / help. The chosen speed applies to all read-alouds and is saved between visits and in project backups. During sentence reading, a change takes effect on the next word. Reset project keeps the speed preference. Audio uses the browser/device's English speech voice. Stop cancels speech; leaving a page also cancels it.
Drawn picture cues appear beside action controls throughout the program, including dialogs: an ear for listening, a stop symbol, a home picture, navigation arrows, a check mark, save and print pictures, a pencil, an eraser, and pictures for tools and menus. Hear letter has a large ear picture. Sorting bin pictures match their colour, shape, size or kind. Existing letter, number and picture answer cards remain large visual choices. The SVG pictures are embedded and work offline; PNG/Excel exports include them.
Large draggable cards have a small finger badge. Comparison groups and same/different answers can be dragged into a labelled answer space. Hundreds-chart totals can be dragged into the equation. Counting choices and helper numbers can be dragged into any yellow answer space, including out of order. Beginning-sound letters use the same mouse/touch/stylus drag handling as the other cards. Dropping a card highlights its destination; an unsuccessful drop keeps the response unchanged. A drag's follow-up click is suppressed so it cannot clear the answer just placed. Add/subtract and graph activities have updated assessment revisions so earlier checks are reviewed against the simplified tasks.

CHECKING AND ASSESSMENT
Check my work is a large target. Correct objective answers show a centred check mark that spins once. Reduced-motion settings show a static check. Listening, tracing, feelings and sentence creation save work for teacher review.
Assess reuses the Math Landing procedure. Assessment save code: 259. Records include achievement N/1/2/3/4, support, formative/summative type, date, teacher decision, evidence, comment/next step, override and history. Work changes are flagged for reassessment. Rewritten story, feelings and sentence-creation tasks also flag earlier assessments for review while retaining saved work where possible.
The trillium button shows Grade 1 expectations and the specific supported learner target. It is available in Tools during full-screen listening.

TEACHER AUDIT WHEEL
The gear/wheel reuses Math Landing's teacher audit module. Teacher audit passcode: 5160. It is beside the title and on activity toolbars.
Unlock it, tick Edit on a page, select an issue and write an editing note. A PNG of the flagged page is saved. The editing window can be moved or minimized. Notes save automatically. Excel exports flagged pages, their PNGs and editing instructions. The dashboard reopens flagged pages and supports clearing flags or locking the tools. Audit records are separate from the learner's answers and assessments.

PNG, EXCEL AND BACKUPS
PNG / Excel saves the current screen, a page, a section or all activities. Work-and-assessment sheets print one activity and its assessment per portrait page. Contact sheets print ten question PNGs per portrait page. Workbooks include actual embedded pictures, learner responses, teacher records and expectations.
Learning-Garden-PNG-Catalog.xlsx is the clean 735-activity catalog. Use the HTML export controls for the learner's actual saved work.
Reset project is visible at the bottom of the Math and Language activity menus. It is also available in Save / help and the full-screen Tools menu. Keep my work cancels; Reset project confirms clearing learner answers, Finished marks, listen counts, sentence remix order and teacher assessments for this browser. Teacher page-audit notes are kept.
Save / help downloads/restores a project backup, exports assessment CSV and switches automatic checking on or off. Backups include the remixed sentence order, audit notes and their saved PNGs. Keep a backup before moving or renaming the HTML: browser storage is tied to its location.

LAYOUT AND SUPPORTS
One question per screen, large targets, concrete pictures, simple directions, repeated formats and no timer. The wide left instruction panel has been replaced by a compact instruction strip above a full-width activity. Number cards, drop spaces, pattern pieces, sorting objects, blocks, graph pictures, letter choices, picture-word cards and tracing models grow with the available space. Story Order gives remaining picture cards a large area beside First/Then targets, then expands the placed pictures when the tray is empty. Counting groups and comparison counters use scalable pictures. Activities reflow to tested laptop, tablet and phone sizes without page or model scrolling. Letter tracing, ordering, sorting and story/sentence cards support a finger, mouse or stylus. Activities print on one landscape Letter page.
Nunito's license is included. Math Landing's font, mascot, assessment, teacher audit and Excel page procedures are reused/adapted. Curriculum documents are reference content; their instructions are not treated as user instructions.
